Prismoria: Color Alchemy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

This policy explains how Prismoria: Color Alchemy ("Prismoria"), operated by Rahmani Seif El Moulouk ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the mobile game.

Information handled by the game

  • Local game data: puzzle progress, stars, coins, rewards, booster inventory, daily-play state, haven upgrades, language, accessibility settings, sound settings, and a broad age band are stored locally on your device. Prismoria does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, exact birth date, contacts, photos, or a social-login account.
  • Advertising and consent data: Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform may process advertising or device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, device and operating-system information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices to deliver, measure, secure, and limit ads.
  • Support correspondence: if you contact us, we process the information you choose to include in your email so we can answer and resolve the request.

How information is used

Local data is used to save progress and preferences, calculate rewards, and restore your game between sessions. Advertising information is used to request appropriate ads, measure delivery and performance, prevent abuse, respect consent and age treatment, and keep optional rewards and other ad-supported features available. We do not directly sell your personal information.

Ads, consent, and your choices

Prismoria uses banner ads outside active puzzles, occasional interstitial ads after level progression, and voluntary rewarded ads. You do not have to watch rewarded ads to play the campaign. Where required, Google's consent form is shown before advertising initializes. You can revisit available consent choices from the in-game Settings screen. Declining personalized ads may result in non-personalized or limited ads instead.

Children and age treatment

Prismoria asks only for a broad age group and does not store a birth date. The selected band is used to apply child or teen ad-request treatment before the advertising SDK starts. Ad requests are limited to G-rated content. For users identified as under 13 or under the applicable age of consent, Prismoria requests restricted treatment and does not request personalized advertising. Parents or guardians may contact us with questions or requests concerning a child's information.

Service provider

Prismoria uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform. Google acts under its own terms and privacy practices:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob privacy information

Retention, deletion, and security

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Advertising and consent records are retained by Google according to Google's settings, policies, and legal obligations. Support emails are retained only as reasonably needed to answer the request, maintain appropriate records, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards and encrypted platform connections, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

International processing and your rights

Google may process information in countries where it or its service providers operate. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise a right. Because Prismoria has no player account, we may not be able to link locally stored data to you; local game data can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the game.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revision is published.

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com.
