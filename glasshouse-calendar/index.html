Glasshouse Calendar

Upcoming Google Calendar events on the Glasshouse Home screen, with the calendars chosen by the TV owner.

Glasshouse Calendar is an optional widget in the Glasshouse fork maintained by Rahmani Seif El Moulouk. It runs on a rooted LG webOS TV. This is the public information page for its Google OAuth app.

Connect and choose calendars

In the Glasshouse dashboard, open Home, Top widgets, Manage widgets, then Google Calendar. Import the desktop OAuth client file, choose Sign in with Google and approve read-only Calendar access. Select calendars and enable the widget.

The widget displays upcoming event titles, dates, times and locations. Access is read-only: it does not create, edit or delete events. The TV needs an internet connection to retrieve events.

Data stays with the TV and Google

Calendar requests go directly between the TV and Google. Upcoming events are held in TV memory; authorization credentials are stored on the TV. People who can see the screen or access the TV's dashboard may see selected events.

Disconnect Google Calendar in the widget settings to remove saved credentials and request token revocation. Access can also be removed in Google Account settings.
