Glasshouse Calendar terms of use

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Glasshouse Calendar is an optional feature of the Glasshouse fork maintained by Rahmani Seif El Moulouk. It displays upcoming events from Google calendars selected by the owner of a rooted LG webOS TV.

Authorized use

Use the feature only with Google accounts and calendars that you are authorized to access. Google Calendar access is read-only. Google services remain subject to Google's applicable terms and account policies.

Software and availability

The Glasshouse software is provided under its repository's MIT licence, including that licence's warranty and liability provisions. Google Calendar is a separate Google service. Event availability and update timing depend on Google, the network and the TV; the widget is not a substitute for checking important appointments in the original calendar.

Privacy and stopping use

The TV owner chooses which calendars appear and controls access to the TV and dashboard. The privacy policy explains the feature's data access, storage and deletion behavior.

Use can be stopped at any time by disconnecting Google Calendar in the widget settings and removing the application's access in Google Account settings.

For support, see the project's public issue tracker. Public support requests should not contain credentials or private calendar data.
