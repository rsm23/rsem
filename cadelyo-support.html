Contact

Email rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, Cadelyo version, the feature involved, and the exact error message. If you attach a screenshot, hide any private amounts, names, receipt details, and notes you do not want to share.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or your expense database.

Receipt scanning and dictation

Allow camera access for receipt scanning or microphone access for dictation in your device settings. You can continue entering amounts manually if you decline.

For dictation, choose a language supported by your device's on-device speech service and install its language model if offered. Some system downloads are queued without a progress percentage. Keep the required system service available and try again when the model is installed. Hold the dictation button while speaking and release it when finished. If recognition fails, include the exact displayed error in your support request.

Review recognized amounts, categories, and notes before saving an entry.

Purchases and restoration

Use Settings to open Cadelyo Pro and restore purchases while connected to the internet. Restoration uses the store account that originally purchased Pro. A purchase on one store is not automatically transferred to the other store because Cadelyo has no shared user account.

Manage, cancel, or request a refund through the billing store. Uninstalling Cadelyo does not cancel a subscription. After Pro expires, saved records remain accessible and exportable; features above the free limits are suspended.

Local records and export

Cadelyo stores financial records on your device. Use CSV export before clearing the app's storage or uninstalling if you want to keep a copy. The export destination is chosen through the system sharing sheet. Cadelyo support cannot recover records that were never backed up outside the app.

Widgets hide amounts by default. You can change this through the widget privacy setting if you want amounts visible on device surfaces.

Privacy requests

Read the Cadelyo Privacy Policy. For purchase-data requests, email rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com. We may ask for a transaction reference to locate an anonymous purchase record.

Aide en français

Pour obtenir de l'aide, contactez rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com avec le modèle de l'appareil, les versions du système et de Cadelyo, la fonction concernée et le message d'erreur exact. Masquez les données privées des captures d'écran.

Autorisez la caméra ou le microphone dans les réglages du système pour utiliser ces fonctions. La dictée exige un modèle local compatible : installez-le si le système le propose, puis maintenez le bouton pendant que vous parlez. La saisie manuelle reste disponible.

Pour restaurer Pro, connectez-vous à Internet et utilisez le compte de boutique ayant effectué l'achat. Gérez les abonnements et remboursements dans cette boutique. La désinstallation ne résilie pas l'abonnement et peut effacer le journal local. Exportez les données à conserver avant de supprimer l'application ou son stockage.
