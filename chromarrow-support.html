For help, contact Rahmani Seif El Moulouk at rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com. Include your device model, operating system, game version, and a short description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or verification codes.

Playing Chromarrow

Tap or drag an arrow to guide it through the board. Campaign puzzles introduce gates, switches, portals, hazards, ice, and conveyors. You can replay levels to improve your time. Daily and endless modes offer more challenges.

Progress and competition

Campaign progress is saved on your device. Optional Google Play Games or Apple Game Center sign-in can synchronize progress and submit eligible scores and best times to platform leaderboards. A friend race code lets others play the same board. You can continue playing without signing in.

Ads and privacy

The game uses ads outside active puzzle play and optional rewarded ads. Advertising privacy choices are available in game settings. See the privacy policy for details.
