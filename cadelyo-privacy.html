Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Cadelyo is an expense and budget application operated by Rahmani Seif El Moulouk. This policy explains what stays on your device and what is processed when you use optional purchases or contact support.

Résumé en français · Support

Expense and budget information

Your transactions, amounts, currencies, notes, categories, wallets, budgets, recurring rules, receipt images, and preferences are stored on your device. Cadelyo has no user account, banking connection, financial-data server, or application-level cloud synchronization. We do not receive your expense ledger.

Cadelyo uses this information locally to calculate balances and budget progress, generate recurring entries, and display your history. Your device's own backup and security settings may also apply; Cadelyo does not operate a separate backup service.

Receipt scanning and voice input

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt. Receipt images and recognized text are processed locally using Apple Vision, bundled ML Kit text recognition, or bundled Tesseract models, depending on your device and the receipt language. Images associated with saved entries remain in Cadelyo's local storage.

On Android, Google's ML Kit SDK separately collects technical diagnostics and usage metrics: device and app information, an installation identifier, processing duration, API configuration and input/output sizes, SDK event types, and error codes. These are sent to Google over HTTPS for diagnostics and usage analytics. Receipt images and recognized text are not sent to Google by this OCR API. This SDK is not used in Cadelyo's iOS app. See ML Kit's Android data disclosure and ML Kit Terms & Privacy.

The microphone is used when you choose dictation. Cadelyo requires on-device speech recognition and does not send speech to a remote AI service. Cadelyo does not intentionally save an audio recording. A recognized amount or note may be applied to your editable draft. Dictation depends on the recognition service and language models installed on your device. Your operating system may use an internet connection to download those models under its own terms and privacy policy.

You can deny or revoke camera and microphone permissions in device settings and continue entering transactions manually.

Optional Pro purchases

Apple's App Store or Google Play handles payment. Cadelyo uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access, and determine when Pro expires. RevenueCat processes an anonymous application user identifier, purchase and subscription history, product and transaction identifiers, entitlement status, and technical information such as app and operating-system versions, locale, and currency. Network requests also expose connection information to the receiving service.

Purchase information is used for app functionality, purchase verification, fraud prevention, and purchase analytics in the RevenueCat dashboard. Cadelyo does not send RevenueCat your expense transactions, budgets, receipt images, or dictated audio. We do not receive your payment-card details. Cadelyo does not ask for a name, email address, or password to use Pro.

Purchasing, restoring, and refreshing purchase status require an internet connection. Subscription access expires at its last known expiration date; a verified lifetime purchase has no subscription expiration. Losing Pro does not delete your local financial records.

Widgets, device lock, and export

Widgets receive a local summary snapshot. Amounts are hidden by default and can be shown through your explicit setting. Device surfaces may be visible to other people who can see your screen.

If you enable the app lock, authentication is handled by your operating system using biometrics or the system credential where available. Cadelyo does not receive or store biometric templates.

CSV export creates a file on your device and opens the system sharing sheet. The recipient and destination are your choice. Once shared, the recipient or chosen service controls its copy. Exports are not uploaded to us.

Advertising and technical analytics

Cadelyo does not include advertising or advertising attribution integrations. We do not sell your financial data or use it for advertising. RevenueCat's purchase analytics and Android ML Kit's technical diagnostics are separate from your local expense ledger.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local records remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. Export records you need before clearing storage or uninstalling. Cadelyo cannot recover a ledger that was never backed up outside the app. Copies in exports or operating-system backups are managed separately by you and the relevant provider.

Deleting Cadelyo does not cancel a store subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through the store that billed you. Purchase records may remain with Apple, Google, or RevenueCat under their retention practices and legal obligations.

To request access, correction, or deletion of purchase-related information we can identify, email rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com with “Cadelyo privacy request” in the subject. Because Cadelyo has no personal account, we may need a purchase transaction reference to find the appropriate record. Do not send your payment-card details, password, or expense ledger. Support correspondence is used to answer your request and retained as needed to handle it and meet applicable obligations.

Google retains ML Kit diagnostic records under its own policies. Service providers may process purchase-related or diagnostic information in the countries where they operate. Depending on your location, applicable privacy law may provide rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us with requests concerning information we control.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when Cadelyo's data handling changes.

Résumé en français

Cadelyo est éditée par Rahmani Seif El Moulouk. Vos opérations, budgets, catégories, portefeuilles, notes et photos de tickets restent sur votre appareil. L'application ne propose ni compte utilisateur, ni connexion bancaire, ni synchronisation distante des données financières.

Les tickets sont analysés localement. La dictée exige un moteur de reconnaissance local et les modèles de langue nécessaires. Cadelyo n'envoie pas votre voix à une API d'IA distante. Le système peut utiliser Internet pour télécharger ses modèles. Vous pouvez refuser les permissions et conserver la saisie manuelle.

Sur Android, le SDK ML Kit de Google collecte séparément des informations techniques sur l'appareil et l'application, un identifiant d'installation, des mesures de performance et des événements du SDK pour ses diagnostics et statistiques d'utilisation. Ces informations techniques sont transmises à Google via HTTPS ; les images des tickets et le texte reconnu ne le sont pas. Ce SDK n'est pas utilisé sur iOS.

Apple ou Google gère les paiements Pro. RevenueCat reçoit un identifiant anonyme, l'historique des achats et les informations techniques nécessaires à leur vérification et à leur analyse. RevenueCat ne reçoit pas votre journal de dépenses, vos budgets, vos tickets ou vos enregistrements audio. Les achats et restaurations nécessitent Internet.

Les widgets masquent les montants par défaut. Le verrouillage biométrique est géré par le système. L'export CSV partage uniquement le fichier et la destination que vous choisissez. La suppression de l'application peut effacer vos données locales et ne résilie pas votre abonnement. Les copies exportées et les sauvegardes système se gèrent séparément.

Pour une question, une demande concernant vos données d'achat ou l'exercice d'un droit applicable, écrivez à rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com. Une référence de transaction peut être nécessaire pour retrouver un achat anonyme. N'envoyez pas de données bancaires, de mot de passe ou de journal de dépenses.
