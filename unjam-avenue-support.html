Include your device model, operating-system version, game version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment details.

Playing the game

Tap a vehicle when its marked direction is clear. Advanced levels introduce turntables, gates, signals, underground queues, delivery stops, and tow rescues. Restarts are free and every campaign level is designed to be solvable without payment.

Progress and online services

Your campaign save remains on your device. Google Play Games and Apple Game Center sign-in are optional and provide achievements and leaderboards; they do not currently provide cloud-save recovery. If sign-in fails, you can continue as a guest and try again from Settings.

Ads and privacy

Rewarded ads are optional. Banner, interstitial, and app-open ads appear only at eligible non-gameplay moments. Reopen available advertising privacy choices from Settings. The first release has no in-app purchases.
