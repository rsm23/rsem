Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nuvi ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Nuvi mobile application and related services.

By using Nuvi, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Food and image data: meal photos, menu photos, receipt photos, leftover comparison images, and analysis results generated from them.
  • Profile and nutrition preference data: calorie goals, diet preferences, cuisine preferences, and language settings.
  • Health and medical data: body metrics (height, weight, gender, date of birth), activity level, diet goals, health conditions (such as type 1 or type 2 diabetes, hypertension, PCOS, IBS, celiac disease, kidney disease, gout, GERD, pregnancy, or breastfeeding), allergies and intolerances, and any free-form health notes you choose to add.
  • Glucose and diabetes data: if you enable the glucose features, the App may read blood glucose readings from Health Connect, from compatible sensors over NFC, or from compatible devices over Bluetooth Low Energy, and may store insulin logs and carb-to-insulin ratios you enter.
  • Account data: if you sign in or enable cloud backup, your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and authentication data may be processed through Firebase and Google Sign-In.
  • Usage and device data: technical logs, app settings, notification token, device-related identifiers, and timestamps needed to operate and improve the App.
  • Purchase data: subscription and purchase status information provided by Apple, Google, or other platform billing providers.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve Nuvi, including to:

  • analyze meal, menu, and receipt images with AI-powered nutrition features;
  • generate calorie estimates, macro breakdowns, summaries, meal plans, diet plans, and health-coach responses;
  • personalize results using your health profile, dietary preferences, allergies, and goals;
  • display blood glucose readings and help you log insulin and estimate insulin doses for meals if you use the diabetes features;
  • store and display your food history, reports, and progress views;
  • sync or back up your data across devices when you enable cloud features;
  • send reminders, alerts, and service notifications if you allow them;
  • manage subscriptions, premium access, and purchase restoration;
  • display ads for users without premium access where applicable; and
  • detect problems, maintain security, and improve reliability.

Photos, Scans, and AI Processing

When you use food scanning, menu analysis, receipt analysis, leftover tracking, travel-food guidance, meal-plan generation, diet-plan generation, or health-coach features, the App may send images, text inputs, and relevant profile context to AI providers for processing. The profile context may include health-related information you have entered, such as your body metrics, health conditions, allergies, dietary preferences, and goals, so that the results can be tailored to your needs.

This processing is used to return nutrition estimates, recommendations, summaries, and other in-app results. These outputs are informational only and are not medical advice.

Health, Glucose, and Diabetes Data

Nuvi includes optional features for people managing diabetes. These features are only active after you enable them and grant the required permissions.

  • Health Connect (Android): with your permission, the App reads your blood glucose readings (read-only) from Health Connect, including historical readings, to display them alongside your meals and trends. The App does not write data to Health Connect.
  • NFC sensor reading: with your permission, the App can read compatible sensors (such as Libre-type sensors) over NFC. Raw sensor memory blocks and identifiers are processed and stored on your device.
  • Bluetooth Low Energy: with your permission, the App can scan for and connect to compatible glucose devices. It may access device names, signal strength, service identifiers, and manufacturer data to establish a connection.
  • Insulin and ratios: insulin doses you log and the carb-to-insulin ratios you configure are stored to help estimate insulin for meals.

Glucose, sensor, and insulin data are stored on your device by default. If you enable cloud backup, this data may also be synced to Firebase. We do not sell this data and do not share it with third parties for advertising. Health data accessed through Health Connect is used only to provide the in-app features described above and is handled in accordance with Health Connect requirements. All glucose- and insulin-related outputs are informational only and are not a medical device or medical advice; always follow the guidance of your healthcare provider.

Data Stored on Your Device

Nuvi stores certain information locally on your device, including food history, nutrition summaries, health-profile inputs, glucose readings, sensor scans, insulin logs, preferences, cached data, and settings needed for core app functionality.

Some technical values, such as notification tokens or app state preferences, may also be stored locally to support notifications, reminders, and smoother app behavior.

Cloud Backup and Sync

If you sign in and use cloud features, your food records, nutrition data, image references, health-profile inputs, glucose readings, sensor scans, insulin logs, diet goals, translation cache, and related metadata may be stored in cloud services such as Firebase.

Cloud backup is optional. If you do not use account-based features, most of your food-history data may remain only on your device, except where data must be sent to external services to provide live functionality such as AI analysis, notifications, ads, or billing.

Third-Party Services

Nuvi relies on third-party providers to deliver parts of the service. These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Services used by the App may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Messaging, Storage)
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google AdMob
  • OpenAI
  • Groq
  • OpenRouter
  • Anthropic
  • Apple App Store and In-App Purchase Services

Ads and Purchases

Nuvi may show app-open, interstitial, and rewarded ads to users who do not have premium access. Advertising partners may collect device and usage data according to their own policies.

Subscription purchases and restorations are handled by platform billing providers such as Apple and Google. We do not receive your full payment card information.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the App may use a push-notification token to send reminders, updates, and service-related alerts. You can change your notification permissions in your device settings.

Log Data

We and our providers may collect technical log data such as IP address, device type, operating system version, network status, app configuration, timestamps, and error details for debugging, security, and service improvement.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Nuvi does not rely on browser cookies in the traditional website sense inside the mobile App. However, third-party SDKs may use similar technologies or identifiers to support authentication, notifications, ads, analytics, and related services.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your Choices

You may be able to:

  • decline optional permissions such as camera, photos, notifications, NFC, Bluetooth, or Health Connect;
  • revoke Health Connect access at any time from your device's Health Connect settings;
  • delete local app data from the App or by uninstalling it;
  • sign out of account-based features;
  • delete cloud backup data where those controls are available; and
  • contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of certain information, subject to applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Nuvi is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of Nuvi after an update means you acknowledge the revised version.
