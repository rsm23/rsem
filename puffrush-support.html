PuffRush Support

PuffRush is a garden rescue game by Rahmani Seif El Moulouk.

Playing

Swipe left and right to change lanes, swipe up to float, swipe down to shrink, and tap to roll. Use two fingers to pause. Campaign adventures, expert routes, Endless Rescue and Puzzle Garden work offline. Online leaderboards and platform achievements are optional, available from Home → Game Services after the age check. Younger players keep their achievements and scores on their device.

Advertisements and rewards

Advertisements need an internet connection and may be unavailable. Optional rewarded ads grant the displayed reward only after completion. Ad privacy choices are in Settings. The game has no in-app purchases.

Delete or control your data

PuffRush does not create its own player accounts or maintain a developer-operated player database. Progress, petals, cosmetics and preferences are stored on your device. On Android, Settings → Apps → PuffRush → Storage → Clear storage removes local progress and settings. This cannot be undone. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local data. This does not delete records in platform game services.

For Google Play Games scores, achievements and gaming profile data, follow Google's instructions to delete an individual game's Play Games data, selecting PuffRush. Google controls that deletion and any retention under its policy. Game Center profile and visibility are managed in Apple device settings and through Apple support.

Advertising diagnostics and identifiers are processed by Google and its partners under their retention policies; we cannot directly erase their records. Use Google's data and privacy controls and the provider support tools linked in our privacy policy. There is no additional developer retention period for gameplay data because we do not receive it on our own servers.

For assistance or a data deletion request, email rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com and identify PuffRush and the relevant platform. Do not send passwords. We can remove support correspondence we hold, or help you reach the platform provider for its records.
