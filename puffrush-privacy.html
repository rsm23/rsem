PuffRush Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026. PuffRush is operated by Rahmani Seif El Moulouk.

Game progress

PuffRush stores progress, scores, earned petals, unlocked cosmetics, settings and language on your device. Core gameplay works offline. The game does not request access to contacts, photos, a microphone, camera or precise location.

Advertising and privacy choices

PuffRush uses Google AdMob for banners, interstitial advertisements between games and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, device information, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and comply with privacy requirements. Google's User Messaging Platform manages applicable consent and opt-out choices. Where required, a consent message is shown before advertisements are requested. You can revisit available choices from Settings using Ad privacy choices. Rewards are granted only after completing a rewarded advertisement.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Platform game services

If you sign in to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, the respective platform processes your platform gaming profile, submitted scores and achievement progress. Your gaming name, leaderboard scores and achievements may be visible according to that service's profile and privacy settings. Signing in is optional for core gameplay. PuffRush does not receive your account password. Manage your profile and visibility through Game Center or Google Play Games settings.

Read Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data retention and control

Local progress remains on your device until the application's data is removed. Removing local data does not automatically remove platform leaderboard or achievement records. Manage those records through the relevant platform account and support tools. Advertising providers retain information according to their own policies. You can restrict advertising identifiers in your device privacy settings and revisit applicable ad privacy choices in the game.

Support

If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. Privacy questions can be sent to rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com. We may update this policy when the game or its services change; the effective date will be updated here.
