Playing the game

Drag left or right to steer. Your squad fires automatically. Collect crew and charge gates, change formation, and use your storm pulse. Replay the field course from Settings.

Progress and ads

Progress is saved on your device. Removing app data may erase it. Rewarded ads are optional and require completion; if an ad is unavailable, you can continue playing.
