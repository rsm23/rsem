Privacy

2026-10-13 · Rahmani Seif El Moulouk

Profiles use avatars, without real names or birthdates. Artwork and learning progress are stored locally and are not uploaded by Pikolumi. Advertising is child-directed and non-personalized.

Google Mobile Ads and its privacy SDK process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, advertising data, crash reports, performance data and diagnostics. App-scoped, SDK-instance or other device identifiers may also be processed for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Child-directed, non-personalized requests disable the Android advertising ID and Apple advertising identifier; this does not disable every SDK identifier or mean that no data is processed. Profiles, artwork and learning progress are not passed to Google. SDK data is encrypted in transit. Parents can open applicable Google privacy options in the protected parent area; the app does not request advertising consent from children.

Local saves remain until you remove the app or clear its data in device settings. Export is a parent action and opens the device share sheet; the parent chooses the destination. There is no cloud account, application backend, chat or app-owned analytics service.
