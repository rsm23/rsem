Quick troubleshooting

  • Restart Prismoria and install the latest available app update.
  • Check that the device has enough free storage and a working internet connection when requesting an ad.
  • If an optional rewarded ad is unavailable, wait briefly and try again; rewards are granted only after the ad provider confirms completion.
  • Review music, sound, haptics, color-symbol, reduced-motion, language, and privacy controls from Settings.

Progress and deletion

Prismoria progress is stored locally on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it may permanently remove that progress. Prismoria does not currently provide a cloud account or cross-device restore.
