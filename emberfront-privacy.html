Emberfront: Last Haven

Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026

Information handled

Game progress, salvage, squad upgrades, language, and preferences are saved locally on your device. The game has no player account or chat and does not request your contacts, photos, precise location, name, email, or phone number. Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform process advertising and consent information. This may include IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, performance data, crash logs, and consent choices.

Advertising and privacy choices

Emberfront uses menu banner ads, occasional interstitial ads between adventures, and optional rewarded ads that grant salvage after completion. Rewarded ads are never required to play. Where required, Google displays a consent message before ad requests. You can reopen available privacy choices in Settings. Availability and ad personalization depend on your choices and applicable requirements. The game does not request Apple tracking authorization or access to the advertising identifier through an ATT prompt.

Purposes and recipients

Local data supports gameplay and saves your settings. Google processes advertising information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain security, and improve ad performance. We do not operate a server that receives your saved campaign. If you email support, we use the details you supply to answer your request. Advertising providers may process information outside your country, subject to their applicable safeguards. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear app data or remove the app, subject to device backups. Removing the app does not delete advertising information already processed by Google; Google’s retention and deletion practices apply to that information. Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and receive a copy of your information. Contact us to exercise rights concerning information we hold. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, including the CNIL in France. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Children

This game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information directly. Parents or guardians who believe a child has supplied personal information through support should contact us.
