Unjam Avenue: Car Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

Information handled

Campaign progress, coins, unlocked themes, missions, preferences, and pending game-service achievements and scores are saved locally on your device. The game has no developer-operated player account or chat and does not request your contacts, photos, precise location, name, email, or phone number.

Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform may process advertising and consent information, including IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices. Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, and Cloud Messaging may process app-instance identifiers, gameplay and app-interaction events, device and operating-system information, diagnostics and crash data, configuration requests, and a messaging token. We use these services to understand game performance, diagnose failures, configure eligible features, and deliver optional game notifications. If you choose to sign in, Google Play Games or Apple Game Center processes your platform identity, achievements, and leaderboard scores under your platform account.

Advertising and privacy choices

Unjam Avenue uses banners on selected menus, occasional interstitial ads between completed levels, app-open ads after eligible background periods, and optional rewarded ads for clearly described benefits. Ads do not cover active puzzle play. Rewarded ads are optional. Where required, Google displays a consent message before ad requests. You can reopen available privacy choices from Settings.

The game does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency authorization and does not intentionally access the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. Advertising availability and personalization remain subject to your consent choices and platform restrictions.

Game services

Google Play Games and Apple Game Center sign-in are optional. Guest and offline play remain available. When signed in, earned achievements and best scores may be submitted to the platform. The game does not currently provide cloud save or cross-platform account recovery; deleting app data or removing the app may erase local progress.

Purposes and recipients

Local data runs the game and remembers your settings. Google processes advertising information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain security, and improve ad performance. Firebase processes analytics, diagnostics, configuration, and messaging information to operate and improve the game. Google or Apple processes game-service information to provide sign-in, achievements, and leaderboards. We do not operate a server that receives your saved campaign. If you email support, we use the details you supply to respond.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information, and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear app data or remove the game, subject to device backups. Firebase, advertising, platform achievements, and leaderboard information remain according to Google or Apple's applicable controls and retention policies. Removing the game does not delete information already processed by those providers. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.

To request deletion of information controlled by the Unjam Avenue developer, email rahmani@seifelmoulouk.com with the subject “Unjam Avenue data deletion request” and describe the data or support correspondence you want removed. We will verify and complete eligible requests, or explain any legal retention requirement. For Google Play Games, Firebase, advertising, or Apple Game Center data controlled by those providers, use the privacy and account controls linked below.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and receive a copy of information. Contact us for information we control and use your Google or Apple account controls for platform data. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, including the CNIL in France.

Children

This game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information directly. Parents or guardians who believe a child supplied personal information through support should contact us.

Purchases

The first release does not offer in-app purchases or a remove-ads purchase.
